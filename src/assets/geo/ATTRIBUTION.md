# Geographic resources

- `land-110m.json`: world-atlas 2.0.2, Natural Earth 4.1 public-domain land
  outlines, redistributed under the included ISC license. No political borders
  are drawn. https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas
- `country-centers.json`: a reduced derivative of the public **mledoze/countries**
  database. Country labels, aliases and representative coordinates are shared
  under **ODbL 1.0**; the full license is included as `countries-LICENSE.txt`.
  https://github.com/mledoze/countries

- The offline lookup in `scripts/data/visitor-places.json` is reduced from
  **GeoNames** `cities15000.zip` and `admin1CodesASCII.txt` (downloaded 2026-11-16),
  under **CC BY 4.0**. City names, aliases, administrative codes and representative
  coordinates: https://www.geonames.org/ ; data and license information:
  https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt
- State/province names and schematic coordinates come from **Natural Earth**
  Admin 1, public domain, from natural-earth-vector commit
  `ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19`:
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-1-states-provinces/
  Where that lookup has no matching first-order division, a GeoNames
  administrative/national capital within the division supplies its schematic
  point. This does not classify a state-level visitor record as a city visit.
- Traditional/simplified Chinese aliases use **OpenCC 1.1.9**'s
  `TSCharacters.txt`, copyright BYVoid and contributors, **Apache 2.0**.
  Source: https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC/tree/ver.1.1.9 ; the license is included
  in `OpenCC-LICENSE.txt`.

The lookup is generated by `scripts/prepare-visitor-places.py`; input SHA-256
hashes are recorded in the JSON. It is used only during synchronization and is
not downloaded by homepage visitors.

Coordinates represent cities, administrative divisions or countries, never
individual visitor positions. These resources contain no visitor information.
The globe and its displayed points are served locally.
