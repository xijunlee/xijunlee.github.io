# Geographic resources

- `land-110m.json`: world-atlas 2.0.2, Natural Earth 4.1 public-domain land
  outlines, redistributed under the included ISC license. No political borders
  are drawn. https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas
- `country-centers.json`: a reduced derivative of the public **mledoze/countries**
  database. Country labels, aliases and representative coordinates are shared
  under **ODbL 1.0**; the full license is included as `countries-LICENSE.txt`.
  https://github.com/mledoze/countries

Coordinates represent countries or provinces, not individual visitor positions.
Neither resource contains visitor information. All resources are served locally.
